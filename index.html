FAQ

COMMON
QUESTIONS.

How do I start a project?

You can start by filling out the project form on our Contact page. We will reach out within 24 hours to discuss your vision.

What services do you offer?

We offer Graphic Design, Branding, Video & Photo Editing, Motion Graphics, Music Production, and Creative Direction.

How long does a project take?

Timelines vary based on scope. A standard branding project usually takes 2-4 weeks, while video production may take longer. We will provide a clear timeline during the discovery phase.

Do you work with clients outside Rwanda?

Yes. We work remotely with clients all over the world. Our communication is virtual and we adapt to your timezone.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes. We structure our projects to include rounds of revisions to ensure you are 100% satisfied with the final output.

Do you edit social media videos?

Absolutely. We specialize in short-form content, Reels, and TikToks optimized for engagement and platform specific requirements.

How does payment work?

We typically require a 50% deposit to begin work, with the remaining 50% due upon final delivery. We can discuss custom payment plans based on project size.
